# Thesis Proof of Concept: Enhancing Lightweight CNN Models with Explainable AI for Detecting AI-Generated Art on Online Platforms

This is the Streamlit app that detects whether a work of art is human-created or AI-generated. This is the proof of concept for our thesis, “Enhancement of Lightweight CNN Architectures using XAI for the Detection of AI Artwork on Online Platforms.”

You can upload an image to see the probability of whether it’s AI-generated by Model A (MobileNetV3-Small + LFAB) and Model B (EfficientNet-B0 + LFAB). LFAB stands for Learnable Frequency Attention Block, which helps the model detect frequency artifacts in the images.
